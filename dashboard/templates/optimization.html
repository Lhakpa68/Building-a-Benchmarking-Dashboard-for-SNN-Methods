Four-criterion method selection, not simulator acceleration. Network size selects a measured inference workload; it is not a weight. Weights express the importance of inference runtime, memory growth, scalability and MNIST accuracy. Accuracy comes from a separate native-learning task with different learning rules; positive accuracy weight therefore creates an exploratory cross-paradigm scenario, not a controlled claim that one simulator learns better. Recommendations are limited to five genuine SNN simulators and their measured backends. All performance estimates depend on the selected benchmark conditions.
