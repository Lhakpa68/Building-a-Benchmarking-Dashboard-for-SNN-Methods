Throughput at the largest size
Neurons simulated per second of wall clock at the top of the sweep. Higher is better.
Fitted scaling exponent
Least-squares fit of log(runtime) on log(N). k ≈ 1 means cost grows in step with the network; k below 1 means it grows more slowly. r² says whether the straight line describes the data at all — where it is low the exponent is not worth quoting, and the interpretation column says so instead.
