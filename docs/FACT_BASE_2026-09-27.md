### How much of the energy proxy is rescaled runtime
Regressing log10(energy) on log10(runtime) across the 55 simulate rows of

### Sensitivity of the energy ordering to the proxy's own constants
Kendall tau-b of each perturbed ordering against the nominal one at
N = 10,000, TDP varied ±30% (38.5 / 55 / 71.5 W) and the utilisation exponent
over 0.8 / 1.0 / 1.2:

| | exponent 0.8 | 1.0 | 1.2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| TDP 38.5 W | 0.964 | 1.000 | 0.964 |
| TDP 55.0 W | 0.964 | 1.000 | 0.964 |
| TDP 71.5 W | 0.964 | 1.000 | 0.964 |

Three of the nine are identical to nominal and the other six agree at 0.964;
the lowest agreement anywhere is 0.964. TDP has no effect at all, which is
structural rather than empirical: it enters as a constant multiplier and
cannot reorder anything. Only the exponent moves the ordering, and only by one
adjacent swap.
